## 1. Inclusion rule

A project earns a row if it sits on the Alanya coastal strip named in the W0-C brief — Türkler,
Konaklı, Avsallar, Payallar, Okurcalar, Mahmutlar, Kargıcak, Kestel, Demirtaş — **and** either

- **(a)** has an operating or planned hotel on the same site, or
- **(b)** is a resort-scale residence (≥ 200 units or ≥ 25,000 m² plot) sold on a hotel-services concept.

Cebeci Group's own projects are included regardless of scale: a developer competing with its own
back-catalogue is a market fact, not a curiosity.

**Rows with no source are omitted, not estimated.** Projects that were found but could not be
sourced to a usable standard are named in §5 so their absence is visible and deliberate.

## 3. What the table actually shows

### 3.0 The Azura price band is worse than F-002 records — `[V]`

SOURCES.md F-002 records a 2.1× spread on Azura's 1+1 across four hosts. This research found **two
further hosts**, and both fall outside the recorded range at the top end:

| Source | 1+1 asking | Size | Currency | Completion the same page states |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Haspo (SOURCES.md 9) | 112,000 | 80–89 m² | EUR | "under construction" — **stale, F-006** |
| Seaside (SOURCES.md 10) | 185,000 | 85–92 m² | EUR | — |
| **Antalya Homes (C-32)** | **200,000** | **81 m²** | **EUR** | **"May 2025"** |
| Alanya-Home (SOURCES.md 7) | from 220,000 | 85 m² | EUR | 2024-05-30 |
| Housearch (SOURCES.md 8) | 239,171 | 75 m² | **USD** | completed |
| **NewLifeTurkey (C-33)** | **from 260,000** | **72 m²** | **EUR** | **30 May 2024** |

`[V]` Six hosts, a **€112,000 → €260,000 range on a nominally identical product — a 2.3× spread**,
and the sizes move in the *opposite* direction to the prices (the €260,000 unit is the smallest at
72 m², the €112,000 unit the largest at 80–89 m²).
`[I]` That inversion is the strongest available evidence that these are not comparable units
mis-priced, but **listings of unknown vintage and unknown unit subsets**, at least one demonstrably
stale. It reinforces F-002's ruling: this must never be resolved to a single number.
`[V]` A **third completion date, "May 2025" (C-32)**, is now in circulation alongside 2024-05-30
and the developer's own "2021-2024".

### 3.1 The residence-plus-operating-hotel format is close to unique here — `[V]` + `[I]`

`[V]` Of the 13 comparators, **exactly two have a hotel on the same site**: New Level Premium (5★,
off-plan) and Goldcity (5★, 120 rooms, built 2007). Every other resort-scale project on this strip
sells *hotel-concept amenities* — aquaparks, spas, concierge — **without a hotel**. Cebeci Towers
and Exodus both market a "5-star hotel concept" and neither has one `[V]` C-30, `[V-s]`.

`[I]` Azura World therefore occupies a format with one live off-plan competitor (New Level Premium)
and one 19-year-old completed analogue (Goldcity). The scarcity is the positioning, and it is
sourced rather than asserted.

### 3.2 No residence-plus-hotel competitor exists inside Türkler itself — `[GAP]`, and it is a true negative

`[V]` Turkish-, German- and Russian-language searches for Türkler hotel-concept residences resolved
back to **Azura World itself** (C-32, C-33) or to pure hotel-booking pages. Of the URLs that
promised a Türkler alternative, one **404**'d (C-40) and one never named its project (C-36).
`[I]` The nearest comparable developments are roughly 10 km west (New Level Premium, Avsallar) and
roughly 25 km east (Goldcity, Kargıcak). This is recorded as a `[GAP]` in §6.18 because absence of
evidence was not converted into evidence of absence — but the search was thorough enough that the
negative result is reportable, with that caveat attached.

### 3.3 Cebeci Group publishes plot areas and never publishes prices — `[V]`

`[V]` Across every Cebeci project page fetched — Azura Park (26,000 m² / 607 units), Cebeci Vista
(35,106 m²), Cebeci Towers (10,000 m²), Arnelya (1,902 m²), Azura Deluxe (12,000 m² / 330 rooms) —
**not one publishes an apartment price.** Arnelya is the single exception (€1,000–€1,300/m², C-03).

`[I]` **Positioning Azura World on price against its own developer's back-catalogue is therefore
not possible from public sources.** Any such comparison would have to come from portal listings of
resale stock, which is a different product at a different point in its life.

### 3.4 New Level Premium is the only project in the set with a published rental guarantee — `[V]`

`[V]` **10 % p.a. for 3 years**, with year-one rent deducted from the purchase price and 3 weeks'
owner use, published in Russian (C-17): *"Гарантированная аренда: 10% годовых на протяжении 3 лет"*.
**The offer page is dated 12 November 2024** — 20 months old at the access date, so it may no longer
be live.

`[V]` No other project in the set — including every Cebeci project — publishes a rental guarantee
anywhere reachable. `[I]` Whether that is genuine market rarity or simply an under-published
commercial term cannot be distinguished from public sources, and the difference matters
commercially. Recorded as `[GAP]` §6.15.

`[V]` **Azura World publishes no rental guarantee** in any of the 23 sources in SOURCES.md or the 46
here. `[I]` For a project whose whole proposition is a residence beside an operating 5★ hotel, the
absence of a published rental programme is the most conspicuous commercial gap in its public
material — but it is an absence in *published material*, not proof that no programme exists.

- **Sourced entirely from the public web.** No 1Çatı repository data was copied into this document;
  see §7.
- **769 apartments / 52,000 m² / 7 blocks × 12 floors / 900 m to İncekum beach** — independently

  completion **"July 2026"**; six portals say June/July 2025. Verified directly by W0-C, not only by
  the research agent: the developer page renders "July 2026" and a price-from of €136,400.
  `[I]` A ~12-month slip the portals have not picked up. Per SYSTEM-PROMPT §2.2 the developer value
  wins the display; both are stored.
- **Developer identity resolved by a redirect:** `siberia-turkey.com` **301s to
  `newlevel-group.com`** (C-44) — direct evidence that "SİBERİA İNŞAATİ" (C-12) and New Level Group
  are one entity.
- **Payment terms:** 35 % down (10 % at contract + 25 % within one month), 65 % interest-free to
  completion (C-11, C-14, C-16); C-15 states 35 % + 24 interest-free months.
- **A fractional product is sold alongside the whole units:** "New Level Premium (Share)",
  **€5,400 for a 1+1 of 51 m²** (C-09, C-17, and confirmed in W0-C's own fetch of C-09).
  `[I]` Quoting this as an apartment price would be badly misleading; it is a share, not a unit.
- **Host data error found:** Haspo (C-10) carries a row "1+1, 55 m², **€10,000**".
  `[I]` Almost certainly a truncated €110,000 or €210,000. Recorded as a host error, not a price —
  and it is the same host whose Azura structural data produced F-001.
- **Marketing projections that are not guarantees:** C-15 quotes "7 % p.a. rental income" and
  ">30 % profit at completion". These are excluded from the table. They are the seller's
  projections, not a contractual guarantee, and conflating them with C-17's 10 %/3-year guarantee
  would be exactly the error this document exists to avoid.

### 4.2 Goldcity Tourism Complex (Kargıcak) — the completed structural analogue

- **211,677 m²** confirmed on the operator's own site (C-25) — *"211.677m2 dazzling structure"* —
  and independently re-fetched by W0-C. **This is the only tier-1 source in the competitive set.**
  The same page states *"5-star facility"* and **publishes no room count**.

- `[I]` The €97,000–€264,000 band is **resale asking pricing in a 19-year-old complex**. It is not

### 4.3 Cebeci Group's own portfolio — the developer competing with itself

- C-01 (`cebecigroup.com/en/projects`) renders **28 project entries** while the group's own copy
  elsewhere claims **26 projects**. Recorded as published; not reconciled. See the developer section
  of the Marktanalyse.
- **The index status column is unmaintained** — it marks **Cebeci Towers "Ongoing"** against its own
  project page's 2020–2022 build window, and **Arnelya "Ongoing"** against a July 2019 completion.
  `[I]` Status on the index is not a reliable indicator of build state, which matters directly:
  it is the same class of error as F-006's stale Haspo listing, but on the developer's own site.
- **Arnelya district conflict:** Cebeci's own index and Turkish project page say **Mahmutlar**;
  GEOLN (C-26) says **Kestel**. Per §2.2 the developer wins the display value; the conflict is
  stored. GEOLN also gives 6 floors / December 2019 against Cebeci's 5 floors / July 2019, and
  prices the same units in **USD** where Cebeci prices them in **EUR** — neither dated.
- **Azura Deluxe Resort & SPA: 330 rooms on 12,000 m²** (C-07). `[I]` Cebeci has operated a
  330-room resort hotel on this coast since before Azura World, which makes the 188-room Azura
  World hotel the group's **second** hotel operation, not its first. The official page does **not**
  state a star rating — `[GAP]`.

### 4.4 The remaining comparators, briefly

- **Konak Seaside Premium / Resort** are two distinct projects by the same builder, routinely

- **Emerald Park** and **ART LIFE Residence** are included as sub-scale reference points with large

## 7. Data-handling boundaries

**New Level Premium.** The W0-C brief authorises this project as a market comparator because a
verified dataset exists in the 1Çatı engagement. **No figure in this document was taken from that
dataset.** Every New Level Premium cell in §2 carries a public source id (C-09 … C-17, C-44), and
the developer's completion date and price-from were re-fetched directly by W0-C. Block-level
sold/available counts and the per-unit price ladder held in the 1Çatı repository are **Ataberk
commercial detail and are deliberately excluded** — that is a confidentiality boundary, not a
`[GAP]`, and it is recorded here so the omission is not mistaken for a research failure.

**Portal listings are asking prices.** Stated at the head of this document, in §2, and again in
every analytical paragraph that uses one. This is deliberate repetition.

**Currency.** No EUR/USD/TRY conversion is performed anywhere in this document. Where a host
published a converted figure without a rate date (C-24), the conversion is discarded and the
original is kept.
