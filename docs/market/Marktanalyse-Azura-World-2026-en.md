## 2. The market — Alanya, Antalya and the western coastal strip

### 2.1 Türkler as a place

`[V]` Türkler is a **mahalle (neighbourhood) of Alanya district, Antalya province**. Alanya
municipality's muhtar directory lists `TÜRKLER MAH.MUH.` alongside `KONAKLI`, `AVSALLAR`, `PAYALLAR`
and `OKURCALAR` — all five are neighbourhoods of the same district, not separate municipalities (G-11).
`[V]` Türkler was previously autonomous: belde status from 31.12.1991, converted to a mahalle by Law
No. 6360 passed on 12.11.2012 (M-34).

`[V]` **Population: 4,949** (2024, ALTSO; rank 21 of Alanya's 102 mahalle; 2,616 male + 2,333 female)
— S-043, read by W0-C directly from the PDF with the sum checked.

> **Correcting a widely repeated figure.** The frequently quoted **7,240 is from the 2000 census and
> refers to the then *belde*** (M-34) — it is 26 years old and describes a different territorial unit.
> English Wikipedia gives 4,798 for 2022 (G-17), close to ALTSO's 4,949. **Use 4,949 (2024).**

`[V]` Alanya overall: **361,873 inhabitants, 102 mahalle** (2024, S-043).
`[V]` Alanya has, on the chamber of commerce's own account, **70 km of coastline, "Türkiye's longest"**
(S-043) — that is ALTSO's statement about its own district, not an independently audited figure.

### 2.2 Position and transport — what is established and what is not

`[V]` Türkler lies on the **D-400**, Türkiye's southernmost and longest west–east state road, running
from Datça (Muğla) to the Iranian border via Antalya, Serik, Manavgat, Alanya and Gazipaşa (M-35).
`[V]` KGM (the General Directorate of Highways) is responsible for a 68,517 km network as at
01.01.2026 (G-09) and carries road 400 in its traffic report as sections **400-01 to 400-37**, of
which Antalya province holds 400-04 to 400-08 plus 400-12/13 (G-10). `[I]` The D-400 is the single
access spine for this coast — every settlement named here is strung along it. That is why "distance
to X" in this market is always road distance on one road.

`[V]` The western coastal strip (Okurcalar, Türkler, Konaklı, Avsallar, Payallar, İncekum) holds
**83 of Alanya's 99 five-star hotels — 83.8 %** (S-043). **Türkler alone 20.2 %, second only to Okurcalar.**
There are a further 5 five-star holiday villages (Tatil Köyü), one of them in Türkler.

`[V]` **Independent corroboration from a second table in the same report**: the summary table
(S-043, p. 98) gives Türkler as `1 | 20 | 4 | 1 | 1 | 27 | 5 | 5 | 32` — 1 five-star holiday village,
**20 five-star hotels**, 4 four-star, 27 certified establishments, 32 establishments in total.

`[V]` **Bed capacity by mahalle** (S-043): Merkez 33,129 (18.8 %) · Konaklı 32,417 (18.4 %) ·
**Türkler 27,775 (15.7 %)** · Okurcalar 25,856 (14.7 %) · Avsallar 12,421 (7.1 %).
`[V]` Alanya total: **604 establishments, 82,449 rooms, 176,008 beds** (2024).

`[I]` **The real point is in the ratio.** Türkler supplies **32 of 604 establishments (5.3 %)** but
**15.7 % of all beds** in Alanya. Alanya-Merkez conversely supplies **283 establishments (46.8 %)**
and only **18.8 %** of beds. Türkler is therefore not a place of many small houses but of **fewer,
very large complexes** — precisely the format a residence-plus-hotel project fits into.

`[V]` **ALTSO puts it this way itself** (S-043, verbatim):

> *"Konaklı, Türkler ve Okurcalar, görece daha az tesis ile yüksek kapasiteye sahip olup, büyük
> ölçekli tatil köyleri ve resort yatırımlarının konumlandığı alanlardır."*
> — "Konaklı, Türkler and Okurcalar have high capacity with relatively few establishments and are
> the areas where large-scale holiday villages and resort investments are located."

**The structural cause — evidenced, but marked as inference.**
`[V]` The Ministry of Culture and Tourism maintains a register of **Tourism Centres (TM)** and
**Culture and Tourism Conservation and Development Regions (KTKGB)** under **Law No. 2634** of
16.03.1982; designation is by decree (G-02, G-03). On this strip:

| Designation | Area | Theme | Decree |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Antalya Alanya Batısı TM** | 1,588.99 ha | coastal tourism | 1989/14499 |
| Antalya Alanya İncekum TM | 261.90 ha | coastal tourism | 1986/10792 |
| Antalya Alanya Alara Çayı Güneyi TM | 290.45 ha | coastal tourism | 1987/12106 |
| **Antalya Alanya Türkler Akyar TM** | 101.04 ha | **golf tourism** | 2012/2901 |
| Antalya Alanya Okurcalar Ortaören TM | 141.90 ha | golf tourism | 2012/2901 |

`[I]` The western coastal band was **designated for coastal tourism by decree in 1986–1989** —
contiguous, state-directed development areas ("Alanya Batısı" alone 1,589 ha) were created there
decades before equivalent land east of Alanya, and Law 2634 status carries land-allocation and
incentive machinery. A second wave in 2012 added golf-themed centres at **Türkler-Akyar** and
Okurcalar-Ortaören. Combined with a flat coastal shelf served directly by the D-400, that is an
evidence-backed account of why large-format five-star all-inclusive stock concentrated there rather
than in the town centre.

> `[GAP]` **No ministry, tourism-board or trade-association document stating a *causal* explanation
> could be found.** The chain above is my inference from the designation record, not a quoted argument.

### 4.1 The key structural finding

`[V]` Of the 13 comparators, **exactly two have a hotel on the same site**: **New Level Premium**
(5★, not yet delivered) and **Goldcity** (5★, 120 rooms, built 2007). Every other resort-scale
project on this strip sells **hotel-*concept* amenities without a hotel** — Cebeci Towers and Exodus
both market a "5-star hotel concept" and neither has one.

`[I]` Azura World therefore occupies a format with **one live off-plan competitor and one 19-year-old
completed analogue**. The scarcity is the positioning — and it is sourced, not asserted.

`[GAP]` **No residence-plus-hotel competitor was found inside Türkler itself.** Turkish-, German- and
Russian-language searches resolved back to Azura World itself or to pure hotel-booking pages.
`[I]` The nearest comparables are ~10 km west (New Level Premium, Avsallar) and ~25 km east
(Goldcity, Kargıcak). This is recorded as `[GAP]` because absence of evidence is not converted into
evidence of absence — but the search was broad enough that the negative result is reportable.

## 6. Buyer profile

### 6.1 A premise in the brief had to be corrected

The brief records that 5 of 7 portals are German-language, one Russian and one British, and reads
that as a finding about the target market. **Checked against `SOURCES.md` and against my own fetches,
that statement does not hold** `[V]`:

1. **What is true:** 5 of 7 **ticket URLs** are German-locale deep links. **What is not true:** that
   those five portals are German-language sites. Each of the five serves English from the same
   database; the English versions of three were fetched and contained the same data (P-49, P-51/52,
   P-53). `[I]` **The German-ness is a property of whoever assembled the ticket, not of the market.**
   Presenting it as a market finding would be a sampling artefact dressed as evidence.
2. **`onthebeach.co.uk` is not a property portal** but a UK package-holiday channel for the hotel. It
   sells room-nights, not apartments — evidence about the guest market, not about buyers.
3. **The Russian-language channel is not one portal but at least four**: kalinka-realty.com (Moscow),
   firstalanya.ru, alanyaagent.com (EN + RU) and cestate.net.
4. **A previously unrecorded channel exists:** **vikingen.net, operated by Tyrkialeiligheter AS
   (Norway)** (P-55) — a Nordic distribution channel absent from `SOURCES.md`.

`[I]` **Correctly stated, the finding is:** Azura World is distributed through an **unbranded,
multilingual reseller network of at least ten independent agencies** across German, Russian,
Norwegian, English and Gulf-facing channels, **with no developer-controlled price**. That network
structure — not a German bias — explains F-002.

### 6.5 Legal framework — what is settled and what is not

`[V]` **Foreign acquisition of property** (Tapu Kanunu 2644, Art. 35 as amended by Law 6302 of
03.05.2012, S-056): foreign natural persons may hold at most **10 % of the privately-owned area of a
district (ilçe)**; nationally **30 hectares** per person, which the President may double. Military
forbidden and security zones are checked against the Defence and Interior Ministry registers.

`[V]` **Citizenship by real-estate investment:** minimum **USD 400,000**; the property must have
**condominium title (kat mülkiyeti/kat irtifakı)** established or be land with a building on it;
**3-year non-disposal annotation** on the title (S-027, TKGM guide). The instrument is officially
confirmed: RG **12.12.2023, Sayı 32397, Karar 7938** (S-053).
> `[GAP]` **The Regulation's own text could not be retrieved** (S-060 returned HTML instead of PDF).
> The USD 400,000 figure rests on a **guide dated 01.02.2024** — about 2.5 years old. **Whether that
> threshold still applies in July 2026 is not established. Re-verify before any client-facing use.**

`[V]` **Residence permit for owners:** the property must be a dwelling and be used as such;
short-term permits for a maximum of two years per issuance (S-062, P-39).
> `[GAP]` **The widely quoted USD 200,000 minimum for a property-based residence permit cannot be
> officially sourced** — the migration authority's own page states **no minimum value at all**. The
> figure exists only on law-firm and agency pages. **Do not present it as a settled legal fact.**

`[V]` **Closed neighbourhoods — and here lies a genuine locational advantage.** The migration
authority closed neighbourhoods to new residence-permit registration with effect from 01.07.2022; the
machine-readable list (**1,162 rows, 63 provinces**, revision 31.08.2022) names for Alanya:
**KARGICAK, MAHMUTLAR, KESTEL, AVSALLAR** (S-066, S-067).
`[V]` **Türkler appears nowhere in the national closed list** — verified by explicit negative search
across all 1,162 rows.

`[I]` **This is an evidenced, direct difference from the comparator:** Avsallar — where New Level
Premium stands — was on the list; Türkler was not.

> `[GAP]` **The current position, however, is unknown, and this is the most commercially dangerous
> open item in the register.** Local press reported a **reopening** of the Alanya neighbourhoods on
> 4/6 June 2026 — attributed to a party district chairman and to trade-body presidents, with one
> report stating explicitly that the official letters were still pending (P-43, P-44). An agency page
> had claimed the opposite eight days earlier (P-45). **Nothing newer than the 2022 announcements
> appears on the migration authority's own sites** (S-063, S-068). **The reopening is a reported
> political announcement, not an evidenced change in law.** Any question about residence eligibility
> for a specific Türkler address must be answered *"to be checked with Göç İdaresi Alanya at the time
> of purchase"* — never from a document.

`[V]` **DASK** (compulsory earthquake insurance): land registries **may not** perform registration or
cancellation for insurable units without proof of a valid policy as at the transaction date
(Law 6305 Art. 11(2), S-079, S-078).

### 7.2 The finding that requires care

`[V]` The entry sits in **Table 7 (Basit Konaklama Tesisleri — simple accommodation establishments

certificate with an official star classification.** The "5★" on booking portals is, at that date, a
brand or portal classification, not an official one.

> **And here is the necessary limitation, so that no false conclusion is drawn:** `[V]` the hotel

> `[GAP]` **Whether an operating certificate with an official five-star classification was issued

> ministry files for facility registers stop at 2017 and 2022 respectively. **The statement is
> therefore: "recorded as BKT at 31.12.2024, official star classification not evidenced at that date"
